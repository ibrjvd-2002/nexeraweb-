FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I have to buy all five products?

No. Start with what you need — for example POS and Operations — and add the website, WhatsApp AI bot or voice agent whenever you're ready. Everything plugs into the same platform.

Does it support multiple branches and a central kitchen?

Yes. Manage unlimited branches, a central kitchen and warehouses, with internal transfers, approvals and consolidated reporting.

Can customers really pay inside WhatsApp?

The bot sends a secure payment link (KNET, Visa/Mastercard, Apple Pay). Once paid, the order is confirmed automatically and sent to the kitchen.

Does the AI voice agent speak Arabic?

Yes — Gulf Arabic and English, with a natural voice. It can transfer the call to your staff at any time.

How long does setup take?

Most restaurants go live in 2–4 weeks depending on the number of branches and products selected.
